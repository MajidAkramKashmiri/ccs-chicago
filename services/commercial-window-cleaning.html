Interior, exterior and frames

What commercial window cleaning covers

Glass is the first thing anyone sees and the last thing most contracts cover properly. We clean interior and exterior faces, and we do the frames, sills and tracks too — leaving those is why windows look dirty again within a week.

  • Interior and exterior glass, storefront to mid-rise
  • Frames, sills and tracks cleaned, not skipped
  • Purified-water pole systems for reachable elevations
  • Partition and internal glass on the janitorial rotation
  • Scheduled quarterly, monthly or to your standard

How it works

How we run the program

  1. 01

    Walk the site

    We visit, measure and look at your actual floors, glass and surfaces. No square-footage guess over the phone.

  2. 02

    Scope and fixed price

    You get a written scope with a frequency against each task and a fixed price per visit. Nothing is billed that is not on that sheet.

  3. 03

    Crew assigned and inducted

    A named crew and supervisor are assigned, inducted to your site rules, badged and issued access.

  4. 04

    Clean, inspect, report

    The crew works the scope and a supervisor inspects against it. You get the report, not just the invoice.

  5. 05

    Review and adjust

    After the first month we review what the building actually needs and re-tune frequencies. Buildings change; scopes should too.
